Reference image: `/home/solo/Work/2_catalog.webp`. Keep the mod menu translucent over the blurred game view. Continue to avoid auto-clickers, attack/reach changes, X-ray, automation that can spam servers, or other ban-risk behavior.

## In game now

- **HUD:** FPS, CPS, Ping, Coordinates, Keystrokes, Armor Status, Potion Effects, Memory, Clock, Direction, Day Counter, Server Address, Inventory HUD, Speed, Combo Counter, Saturation, Item Counter, Biome, Light Level, Reach Display (read-only target distance), Durability Alerts, Target Health, TNT Timer, Pack Display, Waypoint, and Lag Spike Detector.
- **Visual:** Zoom, Free Look, Crosshair Editor, Fullbright, and No Hurt Camera.
- **Utility:** Toggle Sprint, Toggle Sneak, Quick Perspective, and Waypoint.
- **Performance:** Lag Spike Detector, Unfocused FPS Limit, Particle Limiter, optional rolling 10-second Frame Diagnostics (average FPS, average frame time and 1% low), Optimization Mods HUD, and a one-shot Max FPS Profile action. The profile preserves at least 12 render and simulation chunks, minimizes particles, turns clouds and entity shadows off, lowers entity distance, disables ambient occlusion and biome blending, disables VSync, and removes the FPS cap where the installed Minecraft version exposes the option. The integrated profile and diagnostics bring FPS Companion Max into SM Craft Client's translucent Right Shift menu; the launcher separately provides its device-aware FPS Booster and mod installation.
